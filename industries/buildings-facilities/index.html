Built for facilities teams

The CMMS capabilities that matter on site — in the language your team already uses.

Work orders across sites

Raise, assign, and close jobs from reactive call-outs to planned plant work. Comments, time logs, and photos stay on the work order — not buried in email.

Site & building hierarchy

Model sites, buildings, floors, and equipment the way your portfolio is structured. Attach manuals, photos, and QR codes so technicians find the right asset fast.

Tenant request portal

Give tenants a public link or QR code to submit maintenance requests. Requests land as work orders — no login required for the requester.

Asset map

Pin sites, buildings, and plant on a map so technicians see where the job is — useful across a multi-site FM portfolio.

Planned preventive work

Schedule plant and building services by calendar interval. Work orders open automatically when due so HVAC, fire, and life-safety checks do not slip.

SLA & performance reporting

Track response and completion trends for FM contracts. Export PM compliance and work-order history when landlords or clients need evidence.

Full audit trail

Every status change, comment, and checklist sign-off is recorded. Useful when insurers, auditors, or contract reviews ask what happened and when.
