Built for hospitality engineering

Tools that match how hotels run maintenance — rooms, guests, and rapid response.

Guest request portal

A guest-facing link for maintenance requests. Optional QR codes in rooms and public areas let guests or staff report issues instantly — no app install, no login.

Rapid-response work orders

Turn guest and house calls into tracked work orders. Priority, room number, and status stay visible so engineering and front desk stay aligned.

Room & equipment checklists

Standardize make-ready, HVAC, and life-safety procedures. Enforce step completion so critical checks are not skipped on a busy shift.

Preventive schedules

Keep plant, kitchen, and pool equipment on interval. Automatic work orders when due reduce guest-facing failures from skipped service.

Engineering team & certs

Roles for supervisors and technicians. Track certifications and skill expiry so the right person gets the job — especially for licensed work.

Audit-ready history

Every edit, status change, and checklist sign-off is logged. Useful for brand standards, insurance, and property reviews.
