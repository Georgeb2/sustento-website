Everything your maintenance team needs

From reactive fixes to planned maintenance — one tool, no spreadsheets.

Work Orders

Create, assign, and track work orders through their full lifecycle. Status transitions, comments, time logging, and photo attachments — all in one place.

Asset Management

Build a full hierarchy of your assets — sites, buildings, equipment, components. Attach photos, documents, and QR codes. Import hundreds of assets from Excel in minutes.

Preventive Maintenance

Set schedules by days, weeks, or months. Work orders are created automatically when they're due. Never miss a service interval again.

Checklists

Attach step-by-step procedures to work. Enforce sign-off when you need it. Default-on for food and beverage; available for every vertical.

Team Management

Roles for admins, supervisors, and technicians. Track certifications and skill expiry. Match the right person to the job automatically.

Guest Request Portal

A public URL for tenants or guests to submit requests — no login needed. Included by default for buildings, hospitality, and property management.

Reporting

Built-in reports for PM compliance, work-order trends, time logged, asset summary, and team performance. Export to Excel for deeper analysis.

Audit Log

Record every status change, edit, comment, and checklist completion. Default-on for food and beverage; turn it on when other verticals need a compliance trail.

Library

Start from asset, checklist, and PM templates matched to your vertical — so you are not building a register from a blank page.

Security and privacy by design

Built for teams that handle personal data and need a clear audit trail — not a checkbox after the fact.

Authenticator MFA (RFC 6238)

TOTP codes work with Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password, and similar apps. Admins can require an authenticator for the whole organization or for individual users.

Designed around GDPR practices

Your organization is the data controller; Sustento acts as processor. We support a DPA, user anonymization or deletion, and a 30-day retention window after cancellation before data is purged.

Encryption and access control

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access uses authenticated sessions. EU-region hosting is available when you need data to stay in the EU.
